How to tell them apart

  • parser: day and month swap places (7 April becomes 4 July). Any day-first tenant, any channel except the booking-v3 form.
  • hint: the same swap, but it comes in through the form of a booking-v3 tenant. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type month first, and the form reads it day first. Fixing the parser does not change the hint.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, or differs by the reader’s time zone (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). A day/month swap cannot do that. The desk had already listed these in desk-notes.json and filed them under parser.
